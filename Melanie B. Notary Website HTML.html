Notarization Types

Comprehensive notary services for every document type, available remotely or in person.

24/7
Remote Online Notary

Fast, secure, and convenient remote online notarization for U.S. residents in all 50 states.

24/7
Acknowledgment Notary

Acknowledgment is a professional notary service that ensures legal documents are properly executed, authenticated, and certified, offering peace of mind and credibility to clients.

24/7
Affidavit and Oath Notarization

An affidavit is a sworn statement made in writing, typically used in legal matters. When notarizing an affidavit, a notary public is involved to verify the identity of the affiant (the person making the affidavit) and to administer an oath or affirmation.

24/7
Apostille Certificate Notarization

Apostille services are a form of authentication and legalization process used to verify the authenticity of a document for international use. Authorized under the Hague Convention Abolishing the Requirement for Legalization of Foreign Public Documents, commonly known as the Apostille Convention.

24/7
Power of Attorney Document Notarization

A Power of Attorney (POA) is a legal document that grants someone the authority to act on behalf of another person in legal or financial matters.

24/7
Wills and Trusts Notarization

Notarizing wills and trusts involves specific procedures to ensure the documents are legally valid and properly executed.

24/7
Minor Travel Consent Form

A Minor Travel Consent Form is a legal document that allows a minor (usually someone under the age of 18) to travel with an adult who is not their parent or legal guardian. This form is often required by airlines, immigration authorities, or other organizations.

24/7
Federal Apostille Services

A federal apostille is required for certain documents that are issued by federal authorities and need to be recognized internationally.

24/7
General Notary Services

General Notary Services for any document that needs to be notarized.
